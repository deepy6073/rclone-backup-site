Privacy policy

rclone backup client — last updated 24 November 2026

1. What this application is

A personal, single-user command-line tool (rclone) that uploads an encrypted backup archive to its owner's own Google Drive account. It is not offered to the public and has one user.

2. What data it accesses

The application requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope because it must create, list and delete files in the Drive account it is connected to. In practice it only touches:

  • a dedicated folder, backup/workspace;
  • the archive files it created itself.

It does not read, index or transmit the contents of any other file, mail, calendar, contacts or photos.

3. What it stores

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a configuration file on the owner's own computer, with file permissions restricted to that user. No token, filename or file content is ever sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

4. What it does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No sharing of data with third parties.
  • No advertising and no profiling.
  • No collection of personal data of anyone other than the account owner, and none of that leaves the machine.

5. Backup contents

The archive is encrypted with AES-256 on the owner's machine before upload. Encryption keys are kept locally and are not uploaded. Backups contain the owner's own working files; data belonging to other people or to third-party services is excluded by policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Archives stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them; the tool keeps the seven most recent ones. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the stored files can be removed directly from Drive.
